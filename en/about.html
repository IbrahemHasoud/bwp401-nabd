WHY A CAMPUS AGENDA?

Do not miss what could
be your next interest.

Nabd Campus brings activities into an easy-to-browse agenda, helping students choose by day, location, and interest. The idea is simple: see your week before it begins.

An independent Web Programming 1 project supervised by Dr. Basel Al-Khatib. The events, dates, and venues are fictional. This is not an official university platform.
